Ads Map – A Globe and Vietnam’s 34 Provinces

Browse classified ads, rentals, jobs, sales and services on a globe: countries with posts light up; zoom into Vietnam to see its 34 provinces (since 07/2025), then click a province and a ward for posts near you. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Post for free for 24 hours.

How it works

Find classified ads, products, services, and promotions in the exact areas that match your needs with an interactive 3D map. Explore the globe, identify countries with listings, and zoom in on Vietnam to browse its 34 provinces and centrally governed cities under the administrative divisions introduced in July 2025. Select a province or city, then a ward or commune to discover relevant listings for property, rentals, jobs, products, services, and local advertising. Provinces and cities appear as interactive 3D blocks, helping you focus on the locations you care about instead of browsing unrelated listings. Drag to rotate or pan the map, and scroll or pinch to zoom in and out. Zoom all the way out to return to the 3D globe and explore listings in other areas.

Posts run for 24 hours, up to 3 running posts per account, free to post and to browse, no account needed to browse.
