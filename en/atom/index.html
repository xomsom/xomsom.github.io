XOMSOM · FREE 24-HOUR ADS

Post for free, ads that connect everywhere. Reach the right people, with the right needs! Fresh posts every day, opportunities right around you.

XomSom turns the classifieds board into live free ads: every product, service, job, rental or event post pops up in front of viewers in the right province, ward and industry. Posts run for 24 hours, then leave — the ads are always fresh. Posting is free, viewing needs no account.

Posts run 24 hours · up to 3 at once · search in Vietnamese or English

THREE WAYS TO BROWSE

3D chamber, map, industry grid

The same running posts, browsed the way you like — switch with the "View" button in the app, or open each one directly by its own link.

  • Default — 3D chamber

    Discover the latest classified listings and free, visually engaging advertising in an immersive 3D space. New listings appear around a dotted globe, with a comet guiding you to each location and an information card displayed alongside it. Explore new products, services, buying and selling opportunities, and local ads at a glance. Rotate the globe to discover listings across different areas, then select a location to explore by province, city, ward, or commune. Promote your products and services, showcase your business, and reach potential customers with free listings and interactive map-based advertising. Drag to rotate the 3D space or globe, select an area to view its listings, or zoom into the globe to switch to the Map.

  • Map — globe & 34 provinces

    Find classified ads, products, services, and promotions in the exact areas that match your needs with an interactive 3D map. Explore the globe, identify countries with listings, and zoom in on Vietnam to browse its 34 provinces and centrally governed cities under the administrative divisions introduced in July 2025. Select a province or city, then a ward or commune to discover relevant listings for property, rentals, jobs, products, services, and local advertising. Provinces and cities appear as interactive 3D blocks, helping you focus on the locations you care about instead of browsing unrelated listings. Drag to rotate or pan the map, and scroll or pinch to zoom in and out. Zoom all the way out to return to the 3D globe and explore listings in other areas.

  • Industry grid — 21 industries

    Explore classified ads, business listings, products, services, and commercial opportunities across 21 industries: food and beverage, fast-moving consumer goods, beauty and cosmetics, fashion and textiles, retail and e-commerce, technology and software, electronics and telecommunications, finance and banking, real estate and construction, healthcare and pharmaceuticals, education and training, travel and hospitality, automotive and motorcycles, agriculture and aquaculture, logistics and transportation, packaging and printing, furniture and home goods, energy and environment, manufacturing and mechanical engineering, business and professional services, and other industries. Each category appears as a visual block with its own icon and colour, with larger blocks representing categories with more listings. Browse products for sale, rentals, services, prices, photos, locations, and listing expiry times. Select a category to explore relevant listings or publish a new ad in the appropriate industry.

FROM POSTING TO THE FIRST CALL

Post in two minutes, run for 24 hours

  • Sign in

    Tap the "+" button at the bottom right and open the Account tab. Watching needs no sign-in — only posting does.

  • Write the post

    Pick a kind and an industry, write a title, description and price; add up to 6 photos, a video and how to reach you (phone, Zalo, link).

  • Choose the place

    Country, province, ward and street; pin it on the map if you like. The post shows to people watching that area.

  • Live on free ads

    Your post shows up in turn, at random among the others — for 24 hours, with nothing more to do.

  • Viewers reach out

    Viewers click the card for details, open the map, call or message you on Zalo directly.

  • Renew when you need

    In "My posts": edit, hide, run again, renew (another 24 hours) or delete. Expired posts are kept 7 days for renewal.

WHAT TO POST, HOW TO FIND

Five kinds of post, four ways to find the right one

Five post kinds for buying, selling, hiring, renting and events around you. Viewers pick a place, filter by industry and search — they only see what they care about.

  • Product

    New stock, clearance, second-hand goods, local specialities — with a price, up to 6 photos and a video.

  • Service

    Repairs, beauty, tutoring, house moving, delivery… seen by people nearby right when they need it.

  • Event

    Openings, promotions, fairs, workshops, pop-up markets — a 24-hour post suits what's about to happen.

  • Job

    Urgent hires, seasonal and part-time work, jobs close to home — filtered by ward so nearby people see them first.

  • For rent

    Rooms, houses, flats, shopfronts, vehicles, equipment — with an address and a map pin that opens in Google Maps.

  • By place

    Country → province → ward. Click an area on the circle to see posts there.

  • By industry

    21 industries, from food and beauty to real estate and logistics — pick one or many.

  • Bilingual search

    Type "bán lẻ", "ban le" or "retail" and get the same posts — with or without accents, or in English.

  • Smooth on every device

    Vibrant visuals, seamless experience. Easily switch display modes to suit any device.

STRAIGHT QUESTIONS, HONEST ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

What posters and viewers usually ask.

  • What is XomSom?

    XomSom is a free local ad channel — a 24-hour ad flow ("dòng chảy quảng cáo"): product, service, event, job and rental posts run for 24 hours and are shown at random, one by one, to viewers in the right country, province, ward and industry — inside a 3D "atom chamber" called Atom.

  • Does posting cost anything?

    Posting is currently free. Each account may have up to 3 running posts at once; hidden or expired posts don't count.

  • How long does my post run?

    A post runs for 24 hours from posting, then comes down. Expired posts stay in "My posts" for 7 days so you can renew them for another 24 hours; after that they are deleted.

  • Do I need an account to watch?

    No. Open xomsom.com and watch right away; only posting needs a sign-in.

  • How does my post reach the right people?

    Pick the right address (down to the ward) and industry when posting. Viewers watching that area or filtering that industry see your post; they can also find it by words in the title, description, industry or place name.

  • Does search understand unaccented Vietnamese and English?

    Yes. "Hồ Chí Minh", "ho chi minh", "bán lẻ", "ban le" and "retail" all match — kinds, industries and place names are stored in both Vietnamese and English. Shortcut ⌘K / Ctrl+K.

  • Where can I list a room, house or shopfront for rent for free?

    On XomSom: pick the kind "For rent" and the industry "Real estate & construction", enter the address down to the ward and pin the map. The post shows for 24 hours to people watching that area, for free; renters call or message you on Zalo directly.

  • Can I post a job, or find work close to home?

    Yes. The "Job" kind is for urgent hires, seasonal and part-time work. Job seekers pick the ward they live in to see nearby jobs first, or search for words like "hiring", "waiter", "sales", "delivery"…

  • Where can I sell used items or a used motorbike quickly?

    Post a "Product" with a price and up to 6 photos, in the right industry (e.g. "Automotive", "Furniture & home", "Electronics & telecom"). People around you see it within 24 hours and contact you directly — no middleman, no fee.

  • How do I advertise a restaurant, shop or local service for free?

    Post a "Service", or an "Event" for an opening or promotion, with the address and a map pin. People watching that area see it and open Google Maps for directions to you. After 24 hours, renew it in "My posts".

  • Which industries does XomSom cover?

    21 industries: Food & beverage, FMCG, Beauty, Fashion & textile, Retail & e-commerce, Technology, Electronics & telecom, Finance, Real estate & construction, Healthcare & pharma, Education, Tourism & hospitality, Automotive, Agriculture, Logistics, Packaging & printing, Furniture & home, Energy & environment, Manufacturing, Professional services, Other.

  • Can I browse posts on a map?

    Yes. The "Map" view (xomsom.com/en/atom/map/) is a dotted globe where countries with posts light up. Zoom into Vietnam to see its 34 provinces (since 07/2025) as raised blocks, with posts landing on the right province; click a province, then a ward, to get closer. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to spin the globe. In the Default view, zooming into the globe in the middle of the chamber also switches to the map.

  • What is the industry grid?

    The "Industry grid" view (xomsom.com/en/atom/grid/) lays out every running post in blocks by 21 industries, each with its own icon and colour; busier industries get bigger blocks, with photos, prices, places and time left. Click an industry to see all its posts and post a new one right there; the "Industries" panel at the top right of the 3D chamber and the map also opens each industry's grid directly.

  • Will it run on an older device?

    Yes. Filters offer 3 display levels; Low uses simpler graphics so the device runs lighter, with every feature intact.

  • What can't I post?

    Banned or counterfeit goods, scams, "easy work, high pay" lures, sexual or violent content, anything infringing others' copyright or personal data, and regulated trades without a licence. Breaking posts are removed — see the full Terms.
