Classified Ads Map – From the Globe to Your Neighborhood

Find classified ads on an interactive map: countries with ads light up on the globe, and Vietnam opens down to its 34 provinces, cities and wards — rentals, jobs and deals near you.

How it works

Countries with ads light up on the globe. Zoom into a country — Vietnam opens as its 34 provinces and cities (new boundaries since July 2025) — then into a ward, to see ads exactly where you are. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

Ads run for 24 hours, up to 3 running ads per account, free to post and to browse, no account needed to browse. The app interface is available in English and Vietnamese.

Kinds of ads

  • Product: New stock, clearance, second-hand goods, local specialities — with a price, up to 6 photos and a video.
  • Service: Repairs, beauty, tutoring, house moving, delivery… seen by people nearby right when they need it.
  • Event: Openings, promotions, fairs, workshops, pop-up markets — a 24-hour post suits what's about to happen.
  • Job: Urgent hires, seasonal and part-time work, jobs close to home — filtered by ward so nearby people see them first.
  • For rent: Rooms, houses, flats, shopfronts, vehicles, equipment — with an address and a map pin that opens in Google Maps.
