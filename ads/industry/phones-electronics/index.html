Post a phones & electronics ad in two minutes

  1. Sign in

    Tap the account button at the bottom right. Browsing needs no account — only posting does.

  2. Write your ad

    Pick an ad type and an industry, add a title, description and price, up to 6 photos, a video and how to reach you (phone, Zalo or a link).

  3. Choose the place

    Country, province, ward and street address — add a map pin if you like. Your ad shows to people browsing that area.

  4. Go live for free

    Your ad appears in turn, mixed at random with the others, for 24 hours — nothing else to do.

  5. Get contacted

    Viewers open your ad, check the map and call or message you on Zalo directly.

  6. Renew when you need

    In "My posts" you can edit, hide, rerun, renew for another 24 hours or delete. Expired ads are kept for 7 days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sell my used phone safely?

Post real photos, state the condition and any warranty left, and meet the buyer in a public place. XomSom never handles payment — you deal directly.

Can repair shops advertise?

Yes. Post a "Service" in "Electronics & telecom" with your address and a map pin so people nearby can find you.

Is it free to post phones & electronics ads on XomSom?

Yes. Posting and browsing are free. Each account can have up to 3 running ads at once; each ad runs for 24 hours and can be renewed from "My posts".

How do people contact me?

Through the phone number, Zalo or link you add to the ad (at least one). XomSom does not stand in between and takes no fee.
