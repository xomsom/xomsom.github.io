XOMSOM · FREE LOCAL CLASSIFIEDS

Free classified ads — buy, sell and connect locally Post in two minutes. Reach people near you today.

XomSom is a free local ad board. Post rooms for rent, jobs, items for sale, services and events — each ad runs for 24 hours and pops up in front of people browsing the right country, city, neighborhood and industry. Browsing needs no account, and search works in English and Vietnamese.

Free to post · live for 24 hours · up to 3 ads at once · English & Vietnamese search

THREE WAYS TO BROWSE

3D globe, map, industry grid

The same live ads, browsed the way you like — switch inside the app, or open each view by its own link.

  • Live view — 3D globe

    New ads appear one by one around a dotted globe, each with a card you can open, pause or share. Drag to rotate; zoom into the globe to switch to the map.

  • Map — from the globe to your ward

    Countries with ads light up on the globe. Zoom into a country — Vietnam opens as its 34 provinces and cities (new boundaries since July 2025) — then into a ward, to see ads exactly where you are. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  • Industry grid — 21 categories

    Every live ad grouped by industry, with photos, prices, places and time left. Bigger blocks mean busier industries; open one to see all its ads or post a new one.

FROM POSTING TO YOUR FIRST CALL

Post in two minutes, live for 24 hours

  • Sign in

    Tap the account button at the bottom right. Browsing needs no account — only posting does.

  • Write your ad

    Pick an ad type and an industry, add a title, description and price, up to 6 photos, a video and how to reach you (phone, Zalo or a link).

  • Choose the place

    Country, province, ward and street address — add a map pin if you like. Your ad shows to people browsing that area.

  • Go live for free

    Your ad appears in turn, mixed at random with the others, for 24 hours — nothing else to do.

  • Get contacted

    Viewers open your ad, check the map and call or message you on Zalo directly.

  • Renew when you need

    In "My posts" you can edit, hide, rerun, renew for another 24 hours or delete. Expired ads are kept for 7 days.

STRAIGHT QUESTIONS, HONEST ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

What people posting and browsing ads usually ask.

  • What is XomSom?

    XomSom is a free local classified ads board. Product, service, event, job and rental ads run for 24 hours and are shown one by one, at random, to people browsing the right country, city, neighborhood and industry — on a live 3D globe, a map or an industry grid.

  • Is it really free to post ads?

    Yes. Posting and browsing are currently free. Each account can have up to 3 running ads at once; hidden or expired ads don't count.

  • How long does an ad stay live?

    An ad runs for 24 hours from posting, then comes down. Expired ads stay in "My posts" for 7 days so you can renew them for another 24 hours; after that they are deleted.

  • Do I need an account to browse?

    No. Open xomsom.com/ads and start browsing right away; only posting needs a sign-in.

  • Can I use XomSom in English?

    Yes. The app works in English and Vietnamese, and anyone can browse or post. Ad types, industries and place names are stored in both languages, so English searches like "real estate" or "Da Nang" also find ads written in Vietnamese.

  • Which countries and cities are covered?

    Ads can be posted for any country and show up on the globe. Vietnam is mapped in full detail: all 34 provinces and centrally run cities (new boundaries since July 2025), including Ho Chi Minh City, Hanoi and Da Nang, down to ward level.

  • How do I find a room or apartment for rent?

    Pick your city and ward, then filter by "For rent" or the "Real estate & construction" industry. Owners list rooms, apartments and shopfronts with photos, prices and a map pin; you contact them directly by phone or Zalo.

  • Can I post a job or find work near me?

    Yes. The "Job" type is for urgent hires, seasonal and part-time work. Job seekers pick the ward they live in to see nearby jobs first, or search words like "hiring", "waiter", "sales" or "delivery".

  • Where can I sell a used motorbike, phone or furniture quickly?

    Post a "Product" with a price and up to 6 photos in the right industry (for example "Automotive", "Electronics & telecom" or "Furniture & home"). People around you see it within 24 hours and contact you directly — no middleman, no fee.

  • How do I advertise a restaurant, shop or local service for free?

    Post a "Service", or an "Event" for an opening or promotion, with your address and a map pin. People browsing that area see it and can open Google Maps for directions. After 24 hours, renew it from "My posts".

  • Does search work without Vietnamese accents?

    Yes. "Hồ Chí Minh", "ho chi minh", "bán lẻ", "ban le" and "retail" all match. Shortcut: ⌘K / Ctrl+K.

  • Which industries can I post in?

    21 industries: Food & beverage, FMCG, Beauty, Fashion & textile, Retail & e-commerce, Technology, Electronics & telecom, Finance, Real estate & construction, Healthcare & pharma, Education, Tourism & hospitality, Automotive, Agriculture, Logistics, Packaging & printing, Furniture & home, Energy & environment, Manufacturing, Professional services, Other.

  • Will it run on an older phone?

    Yes. Filters offer 3 display levels; "Low" uses simpler graphics so older devices run smoothly, with every feature intact.

  • What can't I post?

    Banned or counterfeit goods, scams, "easy work, high pay" lures, sexual or violent content, anything infringing someone else's copyright or personal data, and regulated trades without a licence. Breaking ads are removed — see the full posting rules.
